## Common Help

- **A station won't play:** Some stations go offline or change their stream address. Try another station, or search for it again.
- **No colors or show information:** Many stations share little information. The colors then come from the audio itself, after a short while of listening.
- **Song identification fails:** It needs a clear stretch of music from the station, and it doesn't work on talk or in the Simulator. Hold the screen again while music is playing.
- **Widget not updating:** Open the app once, then remove and re-add the widget.
