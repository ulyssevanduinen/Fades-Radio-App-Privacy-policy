# Privacy Policy for Fades Radio

**Effective date:** October 26, 2026

Fades Radio ("the App") is developed by Ulysse van Duinen ("we", "us", "our").
This Privacy Policy explains how data is handled when you use the App on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

## 1. Data We Collect

We do **not** collect personal data. The App has no accounts, and we do not operate any server that receives your data.

## 2. Data Stored on Your Device

The App keeps the following on your device only:

- The stations you save to your list, and the colors they last played with
- A short listening history (which station and for how long), used to draw the app's widget
- Your search text, while you type it

We do not sync this data to iCloud or send it to us.

## 3. Network Requests

To work, the App connects to the internet:

- **Station directory.** When you search, your search text is sent to the open Radio Browser directory (radio-browser.info) to find stations.
- **Show information.** For NTS stations, the App asks nts.live for the current show's title and description.
- **Radio streams.** When you play a station, the App connects to that station's own stream server, which can see your IP address and standard connection details, as any internet radio listener can. These servers are run by the stations, not by us.

These services have their own privacy policies. We do not send them any account, device or advertising identifier.

## 4. Song Identification

If you hold the screen to identify a song, the App takes a few seconds of the audio **from the station's stream** (not from your microphone), turns it into an audio fingerprint on your device, and sends that fingerprint to Apple's ShazamKit service to find a match. The App does not use or ask for microphone access. Apple handles this data under its own privacy policy.

## 5. Widget Data

If you use the widget, your listening history is shared with the widget through Apple's App Group container on your device. It does not leave your device.

## 6. Audio Analysis

The App analyzes the audio you are listening to (for example its tempo and whether it is speech or music) on your device to move the colors. This analysis is not stored or sent anywhere.

## 7. Analytics, Tracking, Ads

- No analytics or tracking SDKs
- No advertising SDKs
- We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites

## 8. Sharing of Data

We do not sell, rent or share your data. The only data that leaves your device is described in sections 3 and 4.

## 9. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data stays on your device until you remove it. You can remove saved stations in the App, and deleting the App removes all of its data from your device.

## 10. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed specifically to children, and we do not collect personal information from anyone.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updated versions will be published here with a new effective date.
